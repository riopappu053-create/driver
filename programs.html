Endorsements

Earn more per mile. Add an endorsement.

Each endorsement is a short add-on module with its own written exam and, where required, a practical component. Add them on top of any class.

H

HazMat

ELDT theory, TSA threat assessment guidance, and the written exam prep that opens the highest-paying freight lanes.

1–2 Weeks $450
N

Tanker

Loaded-trailer handling, surge control, and liquid-load dynamics taught on our range with a loaded tanker.

1 Week $550
T

Doubles & Triples

Set-up and break-down drills, coupling practice, and safe handling of multi-trailer configurations.

1 Week $550
P

Passenger

Required for passenger vans and small buses. Covers loading, unloading, and passenger safety procedures.

1 Week $450
S

School Bus

For school bus drivers. Includes student loading/unloading, railroad crossing procedures, and state-specific requirements.

1–2 Weeks $500
Bundle

HazMat + Tanker

The two highest-paying endorsements in one package. Most popular add-on for graduates heading into fuel or chemical hauling.
