Why IronRoute

Not just a school. A launchpad.

We built IronRoute to be the school we wish we had when we started driving. Every decision is based on one question: will this make our graduates better drivers?

Real Miles. Real Equipment.

You can't learn to drive a 10-speed from a textbook. We put you behind the wheel of late-model automatic and manual tractors. Our range has 14 acres of backing lanes, offset alleys, and city mockups.

  • 40+ hours behind the wheel
  • Dedicated night driving modules
  • On-site DOT inspection bays

Small Classes. Big Attention.

We maintain a strict 2:1 student-to-truck ratio. That means less waiting around and more one-on-one coaching. If you're struggling with a backing maneuver, your instructor is right there to help you correct it before it becomes a bad habit.

  • No crowded cabs
  • Instructors with 10+ years OTR
  • Patient, step-by-step coaching

Enrollment Process

Four steps to your new career.

We keep the process simple. No endless paperwork, no hidden hurdles — just a clear path to your CDL.

1

Apply Online

Fill out our simple application. It takes 5 minutes and costs nothing.

2

Get Your Permit

Pass your DOT physical and written CLP exam. We provide all the study material.

3

Train Hard

Hit the range and the highway with your instructor. Master pre-trips and backing.

4

Get Hired

Pass the state skills test and meet with our carrier partners to start earning.
