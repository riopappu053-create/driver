After Graduation
Partner Network

40+ carriers. Six freight lanes.

Our partner network spans regional fleets, national LTL carriers, food-service distributors and government depots. Whatever lane you want to run, we know who's hiring there.

OTR Fleets

Long-haul, cross-country routes. Highest starting pay and most miles — with home time every 2–3 weeks.

12 Partners $62k–$78k

LTL Freight

Less-than-truckload carriers with predictable schedules. Home every night on most routes.

9 Partners $58k–$70k

Food Service

Local and regional delivery for food-service distributors. Home nightly, physical work, strong benefits.

7 Partners $55k–$68k

Tanker

Fuel, chemical and liquid food hauling. Requires the Tanker endorsement and pays a premium per mile.

6 Partners $68k–$85k

Government

State and municipal depots, transit authority fleets. Pension, benefits and a very stable schedule.

4 Partners $52k–$66k
Most Flexible

Regional

Regional runs that keep you within a few hundred miles of home. Best of both worlds — decent pay, decent home time.
