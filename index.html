Questions

Answers before you climb in.

Still unsure? Our admissions team answers every enquiry within one working day.

Do I need experience before I start?

None at all. Most students have never sat in a commercial cab before day one. You'll need a valid driver's licence, to be at least 18 (21 for interstate work), and to pass a DOT medical exam — we'll walk you through that in the screening call.

How many driving hours will I actually get?

Class A students get a minimum of 40 behind-the-wheel hours plus range time, split across highway, night and city driving. Every hour is logged in your student dashboard so you always know exactly where you stand.

Can I keep my current job while training?

Yes. We run evening and weekend cohorts specifically for working students, and the ELDT theory portion is self-paced online. Many students finish Class B in four weeks while still working.

Is financing available, and will a carrier pay me back?

We offer in-house payment plans from $325/month and partner with two tuition-financing providers. Several of our carrier partners also reimburse tuition after 90 days of employment — our placement team will tell you which ones before you sign anything.
