Contact Questions

Before you reach out.

The answers most people are looking for before they call. If yours isn't here, the form above is the fastest way in.

Ask Something Else
Do I need an appointment to visit the yard?

Walk-ins are welcome during yard hours, but booking ahead means an instructor is free to show you the trucks and answer questions properly. Use the form above or call admissions to lock in a 20-minute slot.

How quickly will someone contact me?

Every form submission and voicemail gets a reply within one business day — usually the same afternoon if you submit before 3pm. Messages left over the weekend are returned Monday morning.

Can I speak directly to an instructor?

Yes. Ask admissions to book you an instructor call, or mention it in your message. Road instructors are usually available between 4pm and 6pm once range sessions wrap up.

Do you offer financing consultations?

Yes. Select "Financing Question" on the form or ask for the billing desk when you call. We'll send a written cost breakdown, payment-plan options and any carrier tuition-reimbursement programs you may qualify for.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Just your driver's licence and a list of questions. If you're ready to apply, bring your medical card if you already have one — otherwise we'll walk you through getting one.
