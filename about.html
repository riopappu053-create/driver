Our Story

Two tractors, one stubborn idea.

IronRoute started in 2009 when two owner-operators got tired of watching green drivers wash out of orientation week. They had a combined 40 years and 3 million safe miles between them — and a single belief: nobody should sit in a commercial cab for the first time on a carrier's dime.

So they bought a used day cab, leased a gravel lot outside the industrial park, and started teaching pre-trips at 5 a.m. Sixteen years later that lot is a 14-acre training facility with a paved range, six road units, and an ELDT curriculum registered with the FMCSA.

What hasn't changed is the ratio. Two students per truck. Never more.
